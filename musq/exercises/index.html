Inside the app · X-ray demos

See the set-up that makes the lift

MUSQ fades the muscles to glass so you can watch the joints that make or break a lift, from any angle and at quarter speed. This is the bench press demo, recorded in the app.

  • 01
    Shoulder blades on the benchSqueeze them back and down, lie back, lock them and press without letting them slip.
  • 02
    Grip and lats for the deadliftClose all five fingers round the bar and pull the shoulders down before the bar leaves the floor.
  • 03
    A bar on your backHands just outside the shoulders, wrists straight, elbows down and back, spine stacked before you unrack.
