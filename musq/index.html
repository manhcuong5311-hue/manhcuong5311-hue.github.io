Common questions

Is MUSQ free?

Yes. Every feature is free. The app shows ads, and you can remove them with an optional purchase: a yearly plan or a one-time lifetime unlock. Neither one unlocks extra features. Both only turn the ads off.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads

Open Profile › Remove ads and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. It can take a moment for a new purchase to reach the app.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

On your iPhone, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, choose MUSQ and tap Cancel Subscription. The ads stay off until the end of the year you've paid for. You can also open this screen from Profile › Remove ads › Manage Subscription in the app.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my workout history stored?

Only on your iPhone. MUSQ has no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes your history.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it, use Profile › Privacy choices. To stop apps from using your device's advertising identifier, turn off Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking › Allow Apps to Request to Track.
